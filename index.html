About

I'm a Principal Scientist at Amazon, where I work on multimodal foundation models that help machines perceive and reason about the physical world, to help Amazon deliver packages to customers faster and more efficiently. Before Amazon, I worked on on-device generative AI at Apple, VLM for document and webpage understanding at Salesforce Research, and perception for autonomous driving at Argo AI, and early-career research at Microsoft Research. I hold a Ph.D. from the University of Texas at Arlington.

Focus

  • Multimodal foundation models for physical-world reasoning — physical world understanding that generalizes beyond any one task.
  • World models for autonomous & robotic systems — representations that support planning and action, not just recognition.
  • Efficient on-device generative AI — hardware-aware attention primitives, low-bit quantization and few-step diffusion for models that run on a phone.
